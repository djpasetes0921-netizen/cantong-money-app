How this works when pay is uneven

  1. Pay yourself the lean amountYour household budget is built on your worst normal month, not your best. Good months stop feeling like extra money to spend and start feeling like the buffer for the quiet ones.
  2. Split every payment the moment it landsEach envelope takes its percentage before anything is spent. Tap Allocate when you record income and the money is moved out of reach in one step.
  3. Fill the Next Month Buffer firstAim for one full month of costs sitting there. Once it holds a month, you are spending last month's income instead of racing this month's, and an empty week stops being an emergency.
  4. Then build to three monthsAfter the buffer, the Emergency envelope grows to three months of essentials. Everything above that can go to a real goal.
  5. Never spend from an envelope without moving it backUse Take out, so the number on the front screen stays true. A budget only helps while it matches the bank.

Setup

Starting cash is what you hold right now, before you record anything. The monthly assumption is what the forecast falls back on for months you have not planned yet, and blank means it uses your smallest real month. When money is due to arrive is taken from Money you expect, under Plan.

Where your money is

One line per bank account, e-wallet and pocket of cash. Put in what each holds right now and the app keeps them separate from then on.
